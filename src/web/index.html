Safety and data notice

LOTO Quorum records who has locked a machine and refuses a restart permission until every lock is off. It does not control machinery. It supplements physical locks and your site's written energy-control procedure; it never replaces them.

If this page or the network is unavailable, the physical procedure is the source of truth.

What is stored

A job keeps the machine name, the names you enter and timestamps. Those records expire 100 days after the job opens. The sealed audit file in S3 is retained for the period set at deployment. There are no cookies and no analytics. Your browser stores one thing: your theme choice.
